IMSTA FESTA TORONTO BEAT BATTLE — $1,000 ON THE LINE

OCT 2026 · BY TRAV MANE

Big one for the city. IMSTA FESTA Toronto lands Saturday, October 17, 2026 at York University (Accolade East Building) — a full day of music tech, master classes, panels, and networking. Free admission with registration. And the keynote: GRAMMY-winning Atlanta super-producer Zaytoven, live 12PM–2PM.

The connection runs deep — Zaytoven built the sound of Gucci Mane's empire, and Sean Paine (Gucci's engineer, UXK family) moves in that same circle. This is the room where Toronto meets the industry.

Trav Mane is registered and will be in the building — pull up, network, and talk $50 submissions. This is what the KONNECT is for.

The sound: raw hip-hop poetry over deep analog dub, built with Toronto producer Chaix — the same team behind his single "Double Up." Caribbean roots to Toronto concrete. That's a bridge UXK knows well.

One more layer: Bridgeworks is on the shortlist for the UR XKLUSIVE KONNECT: ON THE ROAD tour. Covering Friday's show doubles as venue scouting — that's how the movement moves.

The “Not On ‘Tor’ Tour” is a run of listening-experience events — DJs and special guests running through his catalogue — because Tory remains incarcerated on his 10-year sentence. There are no personal concert dates on any ticketing platform. Announced stops: New York (kicked off Sept 25), Los Angeles, Washington D.C., New Orleans, Pittsburgh — with Toronto and Montreal named as the Canadian stops, dates and venues still to come.
