NOV 11, 2026 · BY TRAV MANE

What do you need before you submit?
Your best song — one, not ten. A streaming link that plays on any phone. One clear photo of you. Your artist name spelled exactly how you want it printed, and your social links. That's the whole kit.

How does a submission work at UrbanXklusives?
You send your music through the submission form. The entry is $50. Every submission gets a website post — your song, your photo, your story, your links — plus a social blast across the UXK pages. That's what the $50 buys: the post and the push.

Does the $50 get me on the mixtape?
No — and anybody who promises that is lying to you. The $50 is the website-submission entry fee only. Mixtape placements on UR XKLUSIVE KONNECT are offered separately, in registration windows. Vol. 1 is out. Vol. 2 and 3 are coming. The post is the first step; the tape is earned after.

What makes a submission stand out?
Real footage beats a still photo every time. A short video of you performing, a clean visual, a story behind the song — that moves. Send your strongest track, not your newest one. And keep your links working: a dead link is the fastest way to get skipped.

What if I want more than a post?
The gigs go further. Social Promo Pack ($50) pushes your post across the networks. Artist Spotlight ($75) is a full feature. Video Premiere ($100) launches your video on the site. Book them on Upwork, Fiverr, or right here through the site.
