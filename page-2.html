"JANICE STFU" IN COURT — PRODUCER SUES DRAKE, SAYS HE HAS THE STEMS

OCT 19, 2026 · BY TRAV MANE

Grammy-winning producer Aviel Calev Hirschfield — "Avi," aka GoodbyeCalev, the man who co-wrote Beyoncé's "Heated" — filed a federal lawsuit on Oct 16 claiming the "Janice STFU" hook is his. His story: he wrote an eight-line chorus in Dec 2025/Jan 2026 over an FnZ instrumental, inspired by Lykke Li's "I Follow Rivers" on a drive to Seattle, sent the demo to Drake's circle, heard nothing — then heard it on ICEMAN in May. He says his stems and metadata prove he was first.

Drake's camp came back swinging — "categorically, unequivocally denied," his rep told TMZ: "There is not one iota of truth to these accusations... Drake wrote this song and has no connection or knowledge of this person, full stop." They also say Avi's metadata was doctored and hit him with a cease-and-desist over his authorship claims. Classic receipts war: stems vs. metadata.

Let's be clear — no court has decided anything. But the stakes are real: "Janice STFU" was a two-week Hot 100 No. 1 with 20 weeks atop Hot Rap Songs and 300M+ Spotify streams. This is why every producer needs to hear this: register your work, timestamp everything, keep your stems. Play the record above and decide for yourself. 🎚️

OCT 19, 2026 · BY TRAV MANE

Every Toronto artist you know walks a road Maestro paved. The trophy itself plays the song. This year's SOCAN Achievement Award trophies feature five bronze crotales — tuned to actual notes from "Let Your Backbone Slide." A trophy that is literally an instrument, carrying his melody. You can't make this up: Canadian music history, made playable.

Trav's take: before Drake, before The Weeknd, before Tory — there was Wes. Toronto stand up. This is your godfather getting his due, and every young artist in this city owes him a thank you.

BEFORE GTA 6 — THE WHOLE WORLD IS WAITING, AND THE SEARCH NUMBERS PROVE IT

NOV 19, 2026 · BY TRAV MANE

🎬 WATCH THE SHORT. 60 seconds, every question answered — real GTA 6 trailer footage, Kodak Black on the soundtrack, the search numbers. Out now.

The whole world is waiting on GTA 6 — and the search numbers prove it. Google Trends just dropped the receipts: everybody's searching the same questions, cleaning the same consoles, and calling in sick on the same day. So we answered every last one of them. In order. Let's go.

🎬 WATCH THE OFFICIAL TRAILER. Rockstar's own GTA VI Trailer 2 — Lucia and Jason, Leonida, Vice City. This is the game, straight from the source.

BEFORE GTA

Q: What's the hype with "before gta 6"?

A: It's the whole internet holding its breath. Searches for "before gta 6" are up 250% in 2026 — and the joke writes itself, because people have been saying "we'll get everything before GTA 6" for years now. The wait became the meme, and the meme became the moment. GTA 6 is out Thursday, November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Q: What did we get "before GTA 6"?

A: According to five years of searches, the top answer is flying cars. That's the number-one thing people said we'd get before GTA 6. Flying cars. A whole decade of waiting turned into the longest-running joke on the internet — and now the punchline is finally here. The game is out now. The flying cars are still TBD.

Q: How do I clean my PS5?

A: Everybody's asking — searches to clean a PS5 are at an all-time high, and "clean ps5" and "clean xbox" searches more than doubled since the release date got announced. Here's the quick version:
1. Power it all the way off and unplug it.
2. Wipe the outside with a soft, dry microfibre cloth. No liquids, no sprays on the console.
3. Hit the vents with short bursts of compressed air, can held upright.
4. The side panels pop off if you want to go deeper — gently vacuum the dust catchers and fan area.
5. Put it back where the air can move. A clean console is a quiet console.

Q: How do I clean my Xbox?

A: Same energy, different box:
1. Power off, unplug.
2. Soft, dry cloth on the outside. Nothing wet.
3. Vacuum the vents gently with a brush attachment, or short bursts of compressed air.
4. Don't block the vents — the Series X breathes out the top, so give it room.
5. Done. Your Xbox is ready for 212 GB of Vice City.

Q: How do I not die before GTA 6?

A: Real talk — this was one of the top trending "how to" searches, and we respect it. The whole plan: drink water. Sleep. Eat something that isn't chips. Go outside for ten minutes. Stretch between sessions. That's it. That's the whole list. The game is out now. You're in.

Q: How do I reset my dopamine before GTA 6 launches?

A: Another top trending how-to, and honestly? Fair question. The reset is simple: put the phone down for a bit. Stop doomscrolling the countdown. Go outside, touch grass, talk to a real human. Let the game be the reward, not the tenth tab you refresh. You're in Vice City now.

Q: How long does it take to beat GTA 5?

A: Searches for "how long to beat gta 5" spiked 1,100% since the release-date announcement — everybody's running it back. The real numbers, from HowLongToBeat (player-logged averages):
Main story: about 32 hours — all 69 missions, Michael, Franklin and Trevor, start to finish.
Main story + extras: about 51.5 hours — story plus side missions, Strangers and Freaks, hobbies.
Completionist (100%): about 89.5 hours — everything the 100% screen tracks, collectibles and all. The slowest runs on record go past 130 hours.
So if you'd started earlier in the week, you could've rolled credits before GTA 6 dropped. The 100% run? That's a different conversation.

Q: Why is everyone searching "beat gta 5" right now?

A: Because the replay wave is real. "Beat gta 5" searches are the highest they've been since the game's release year. Everybody wants the story fresh in their head before the new one lands. Los Santos one more time, then Vice City.

A DECADE LATER

Q: What was 2013 like when GTA V dropped?

A: A whole different world. GTA V was the top trending video game of 2013. High low skirts and peplum tops were peak fashion. The Harlem Shake was the top meme of the year. "Selfie" was the word on the rise. And the week GTA 5 dropped — September 13, 2013 — the top searched songs were Miley Cyrus "Wrecking Ball," Lorde "Royals," and Pharrell Williams and Robin Thicke "Blurred Lines." Thirteen years later, we're all doing it again.

Q: What does the fox say?

A: Ring-ding-ding-ding-dingeringeding. In September 2013, everybody on the internet was asking it, thanks to Norwegian comedy duo Ylvis and their viral song "The Fox (What Does the Fox Say?)" — a joke track that became one of the biggest songs of the year. The fox says a lot of things. None of them are words. That's the whole joke, and it still works.

Q: What time does GTA 6 come out? What time can I play GTA 6?

A: The two biggest questions on the internet right now. "What time does gta 6 come out" is up 1,950% in 2026 and up 4,000% this past month; "what time can i play gta 6" is up 1,300%. Here's the answer:
The date is Thursday, November 19, 2026 — confirmed by Rockstar. PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version announced.
The unlock was midnight local time — Rockstar Support's word, backed by the store listings. For Toronto, that was midnight Eastern — the night of Wednesday, November 18 rolling into Thursday. It's out now.
It rolled out by region, New Zealand first. There was no single worldwide hour — your midnight was your midnight.
Already pre-loaded? You're in. Not yet? Grab it from the official stores below — see WHERE TO BUY.

Q: Can I still get a GTA 6 physical copy?

A: Yes — and searches for "gta 6 physical copy" are up 2,350%. But know what you're buying: the physical Standard Edition ($109.99 CAD) is code-in-a-box — there is no disc. You get a box with a download code, redeem it, and download the game (pre-load from the box has been open since November 12). The Ultimate Edition ($139.99 CAD) is digital only. And if you're searching for a "Deluxe" edition — there isn't one. Standard vs. Ultimate, that's the whole shelf. Pre-orders opened June 25, 2026, and every pre-order and purchase through launch came with the Vintage Vice City Pack.

WHERE TO BUY

Ready to lock in? Here's where to get it in Canada — official stores only:
PlayStation Store (PS5): Standard Edition — $109.99 CAD
PlayStation: GTA VI game page
Xbox Store: Standard Edition — $109.99 CAD

Rockstar Store: Buy GTA VI direct from Rockstar

Prefer the box? The code-in-a-box Standard Edition is at Best Buy, Walmart, Amazon.ca and EB Games (GameStop in Canada) too — same $109.99, same code, no disc.

Buy ONLY from the official stores linked above — PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, Rockstar Store — or the major retailers named here. If a deal looks too cheap or the site isn't one of those, walk away.

THE SOUNDTRACK

Q: Is Kodak Black on the GTA 6 soundtrack?

A: Yes. "Let's Rock" — Kodak Black & Don Toliver is on Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, the 34-track official soundtrack that drops with the game on November 19. NME also lists Kodak's "Skrilla" as confirmed for the game. Read Rockstar's official album announcement. (No official "Let's Rock" video is out yet — we'll embed it here the minute it drops.)

TIME TO PLAY

Q: How many people will call in sick for GTA 6?

A: Nobody knows — and that's the honest answer. There is no hard number. But the search itself is the story: "how many people will call in sick for gta 6" was a breakout search this past week, and the top trending "sick day" searches were GTA 6. The GTA flu is real, and it's going around. If your whole office is suddenly sick, now you know why.

Q: What are the best excuses to call out of work?

A: Another breakout search, so here's the UXK list — playful, not professional advice:
1. The GTA flu. Suddenly you can't keep anything down. Except snacks. And water. And 12 straight hours of gameplay.
2. Food poisoning. The all-timer. Nobody questions it. Nobody wants details.
3. Car trouble. It won't start. What can you do? (It starts fine. You're just not going anywhere.)
4. The honest one. Book the day off. Your boss plays too. Everybody knows why you're out.

Q: How do I clear Xbox storage?

A: A breakout search, because GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB. The quick clear-out:
1. Settings → System → Storage — see what's actually eating the space.
2. Uninstall the games you finished. Your saves live in the cloud, so they'll be there when you come back.
3. Move games you're keeping to an external USB drive instead of deleting them.
4. Check for old captures and clips — they add up.
5. 212 GB free, minimum. Then you're ready.

Q: How do I preload GTA 6?

A: Another breakout — and good news, it's been open a while:
PS5: pre-load opened November 10 at midnight local time. Find GTA 6 in your library and download.
Xbox Series X|S: pre-load opened November 10 at 12:00 AM GMT (7 PM Eastern on November 9). My games & apps → GTA 6 → install.
Code-in-box: redeem the code first (open since November 12), then download.
You need up to 212 GB free. And no — a finished pre-load didn't let you play early. The files stayed locked until midnight.
