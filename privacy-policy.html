Privacy Policy

urbanxklusives.com · operated by UrbanXklusives (Trav Mane), Brampton, Ontario, Canada · Last updated November 9, 2026

This page explains, in plain language, what information urbanxklusives.com collects, why, and what we do with it. We keep this short because our data footprint is small: this is a music, culture and community site with a submission form, e-book orders by email, and a launch list.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. If you submit through the $50 submission form, email us about an e-book order, or join a launch list, we receive the details you provide — your name, email address, artist and social links, and song or video links. We use it only to review and post your submission, fulfil your order, or answer you.
  • Nothing else, by default. We do not require accounts. We do not collect payment information on this site — payments are handled by our payment processors (see below).
  • GoFundMe. Donations to our fundraiser are handled by GoFundMe. Their privacy policy applies to anything you share there, not this page.

Cookies and tracking

  • Google Analytics. We use Google Analytics (GA4) to measure traffic — which pages people visit and how long they stay. It uses cookies and does not identify you personally to us.
  • Google AdSense and third-party advertising. Google AdSense serves ads on this site. Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. Our ads.txt file at this domain identifies the authorized seller of our ad inventory.
  • Embedded players and buttons. Embedded YouTube, TikTok and social players and buttons may set their own cookies — their privacy policies apply when you use them.

Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links — including Amazon Associates and links through our affiliate partners. If you buy through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. That never changes what we recommend or what we charge.

Payments

Payments, when enabled, are processed securely by Stripe and, for crypto where offered, by NOWPayments. We never see or store your card numbers. E-book orders placed by email are fulfilled by email after payment is confirmed.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell or rent your information. Submission details are used only to review, post, or answer the submission you sent. Messages and orders are kept only as long as needed to handle the request and maintain ordinary business records.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what information of yours we hold, or ask us to delete it, by emailing urbanxklusives@gmail.com. We will confirm by reply. You can also turn off cookies in your browser settings, though some embedded players may not work as well without them.

Contact
